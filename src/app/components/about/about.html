Marketing & Distribution Partner

Triputi Pharma is a pharmaceutical marketing and distribution company headquartered in Sector 39, Gurugram. For over 13 years, we have been a dependable distribution partner for pharmacies, hospitals, clinics, healthcare institutions, and medical professionals across Haryana, Delhi NCR, Rajasthan, and Gujarat.

We represent a curated portfolio of established healthcare brands — including Troikaa (Novogen), Syndicate Medicare, Synthokind, Sunlife, and Microlife — offering a comprehensive range of 2000+ quality medicines and healthcare products through a reliable, service-led distribution network.
